Loan Aggregation MVP Proposal

This proposal converts your business requirements into a practical MVP delivery plan focused on high-quality lead generation, customer clarity, and backend operational readiness.

Business Objectives

Primary Goals

  • Generate qualified loan leads from website traffic.
  • Capture personal + financial profile data required for sales qualification.
  • Help users understand eligibility through indicative credit and loan info.
  • Allow users to express bank/lender preference for follow-up conversion.

Phase-1 Boundaries

  • No direct bank/NBFC disbursal integrations.
  • No automated approval decisions.
  • Static/semi-dynamic lender information acceptable.
  • Manual internal team processing for lead closure.

Key Assumptions and Risks

Assumptions

  • Client provides lender/content data in agreed format.
  • Legal copy for privacy/consent is finalized before launch.
  • Internal team is available for lead operations training.

Risk Controls

  • Sensitive data masking and access controls.
  • Indicative disclaimers for rates, EMI, and eligibility.
  • Manual decisioning to avoid wrong auto-approvals.
